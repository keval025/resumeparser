FAQ

Who can see my resume?

Only you. Each account's resumes, scores and files are stored separately and are only reachable after you sign in. Files are kept in private storage and downloaded through links that expire after 10 minutes.

Will the tailored resume make things up?

No. Rewrites may only rephrase what is already in your resume. New numbers or skills become placeholders like [X%] that you must confirm, and downloads are blocked until you do.

How is the score calculated?

Mostly by deterministic rules: required and preferred skills, relevant experience, years, education, keywords and resume quality, each with a visible number of points. AI is used to read the documents and to rate how relevant each job you held is.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, at any time. In Settings you can download everything we store, choose to auto-delete resumes after 30 or 90 days, or delete your account and all files permanently.

What happens when I reach the free limit?

That feature pauses until the 1st of next month; everything you already created stays available.

Which files can I upload?

Resumes as PDF (up to 10 MB and 10 pages). Job descriptions as pasted text, a link, or a .txt, .pdf or .docx file.
